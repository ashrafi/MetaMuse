## Brand & Style

This design system expresses radiant optimism, human-centric intelligence, and effortless vitality. Designed for a personal superintelligence companion, the visual environment moves away from cold, mechanical minimalism or intimidating sci-fi aesthetics in favor of warmth, emotional presence, and intuitive clarity. 

The aesthetic philosophy is rooted in a blend of **Soft Tactility** and **Luminous Glassmorphism**:
- **Radiant and Warm:** Gentle, sun-infused peach tones, creamy blush surfaces, and blooming coral highlights create an environment that feels welcoming and emotionally restorative.
- **Supportive Intelligence:** Interfaces feel alive, attentive, and responsive without overwhelming cognitive load.
- **Pill-Centric Fluidity:** Friendly, rounded geometry pairs with light, glowing cast shadows to produce a tactile, soft-cushion visual language.
- **High Clarity:** Soft backgrounds are grounded by deep espresso and rich charcoal typography to ensure immediate readability and accessible comfort.

## Colors

The palette establishes an uplifting, sun-dappled hierarchy centered on warm, skin-adjacent pastels, energetic living corals, and organic restorative accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#FF6F61` / Warm Coral):** The energetic heartbeat of the system. Used for core interactive moments, glowing focal points, primary action triggers, and active conversational states. Accompanied by `#FF8A7A` for hover glows and `#FF7B6B` for active states.
- **Secondary (`#4ECDC4` / Crisp Mint & Sage `#3EB489`):** Represents clarity, analytical balance, success states, and cognitive synthesis. Provides an organic, cool counterbalance to coral heat.
- **Tertiary (`#FBBF24` / Sunny Amber):** Evokes discovery, moments of delight, inspiration alerts, and contextual highlights.
- **Neutral Core (`#2C2023` / Deep Espresso):** Replaces harsh standard blacks. Infused with a faint plum-warm undertone to ensure stark legibility while harmonizing with blush surfaces. Secondary neutral typography utilizes `#4A3E42` (Warm Slate Charcoal).
- **Surface Canvas System:** Multi-tiered creamy blush backdrops:
  - Base Canvas: `#FFF8F6`
  - Subtle Elevation / Container: `#FFF0EC`
  - Recessed / Emphasized Containers: `#FDEDE8`
  - Floating Highlight Surface: `#FFFFFF`

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to achieve an open, friendly, and contemporary personality. Its geometric curves and rounded terminals mirror the pill-shaped visual motifs of the UI.

- **Headlines & Display:** Set tightly with negative letter spacing and robust font weights (700–800) to feel confident, intimate, and conversational.
- **Body:** Uses regular weight (400) with generous line heights (1.5–1.6x) to make reading superintelligence insights, personal logs, and generative suggestions comfortable and scanning effortless.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Rendered with medium-to-bold weights (600–700) and slight positive letter spacing to maintain crisp legibility on pill badges, metadata tags, and small utility triggers.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial model relies on a responsive, breathable 12-column fluid grid on large viewports, transitioning to a single-column layout with safe-zone inset framing on mobile screens.

### Spatial Principles
- **Generous Breathing Room:** The interface avoids visual density. Information chunks and AI dialog bubbles are isolated with purposeful white (blush) space, allowing each recommendation or thought stream room to resonate.
- **Rhythm:** Standard base-8 spacing hierarchy governs component interiors, with micro padding governed by `0.25rem` and `0.5rem` tokens.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` margins, `1rem` gutters. Floating components conform to bottom navigation anchors and edge-to-edge pill cards.
  - **Tablet (768px–1024px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` margins, `1.5rem` gutters.
  - **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column layout, constrained to a maximum content container width of `1280px` with `3rem` margins.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than relying on dark, sharp drop shadows or sterile grey boundaries, depth is articulated through **Warm Ambient Blooms** and **Tonal Translucency**.

### Elevation Scales
- **Base Level (Canvas):** Flat `#FFF8F6`, grounding the primary interface.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Insets / Recessed Fields):** Rendered in `#FDEDE8` with no drop shadow, demarcated purely by soft color variation.
- **Level 2 (Cards & Modules):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or high-opacity blush surfaces with an ultra-soft, warm ambient shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(255, 111, 97, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(74, 62, 66, 0.03);`
- **Level 3 (Floating Controls, Overlays & Prompts):** Translucent frosted layers (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), combined with a warm coral bloom shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(255, 111, 97, 0.16), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(44, 32, 35, 0.04);`
- **Level 4 (Active Superintelligence Aura / Hero States):** Multi-layered glowing rings using diffused radial backdrops with primary coral (`#FF6F61`) and mint (`#4ECDC4`) highlights.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Pill-Shaped (Level 3)** geometry. Every interactive element, container border, input frame, and badge prioritizes smooth, generous curves:

- **Buttons & Chips:** Full circular radiuses (`border-radius: 9999px`) provide physical warmth, inviting touch and interaction.
- **Cards & Modal Sheets:** Sculpted with prominent, friendly radiuses (`2rem` on standard cards, scaling up to `3rem` on large feature sheets and dashboards).
- **Micro Elements (Icons, Checkboxes, Toggles):** Fully rounded pill silhouettes that eliminate aggressive 90-degree corners throughout the experience.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Full pill shape (`border-radius: 9999px`). Background is vibrant warm coral (`#FF6F61`), text is white (`#FFFFFF`), with an internal padding of `space-sm` vertical by `space-lg` horizontal. Emits a coral glow shadow (`0 6px 18px -2px rgba(255, 111, 97, 0.35)`). On hover, shifts to `#FF8A7A` with increased bloom.
- **Secondary Button:** Translucent mint-tinted fill (`rgba(78, 205, 196, 0.12)`), text in `#3EB489` (or high-contrast dark sage), no border, full pill curvature.
- **Ghost/Tertiary Button:** Transparent background, text in `#2C2023`, with subtle hover transition into `#FFF0EC`.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped modules with `0.375rem` vertical and `0.875rem` horizontal padding.
- Neutral category chips use `#FFF0EC` background with `#4A3E42` text.
- Active AI status chips combine sunny amber (`#FBBF24`) or gentle mint (`#4ECDC4`) with subtle 8px pulsing status dots.

### Input Fields
- Enclosed within pill or high-radius rounded enclosures (`1.5rem`).
- Background fill uses pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or soft container tint (`#FFF0EC`).
- Borders are nonexistent at rest; focus states trigger a `2px` luminous ring in `#FF8A7A` paired with a soft coral ambient bloom. Placeholder text sits in `#4A3E42` at 60% opacity.

### Cards
- Surface: Crisp `#FFFFFF` against canvas `#FFF8F6`.
- Border-radius: `2rem`.
- Padding: `space-lg` to `space-xl`.
- Subtle warm boundary: `1px solid rgba(255, 111, 97, 0.08)` to maintain definition against bright screens.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Radios are full circles; checkboxes carry an extra-soft `6px` radius.
- Inactive state: `#FDEDE8` background with `#FF7B6B` borders.
- Checked state: Smooth spring animation filling with `#FF6F61` and a crisp white inner icon.

### Conversational AI Thought Bubble (Domain-Specific)
- Asymmetrical pill-like speech vessels with custom radii (e.g., `2rem 2rem 2rem 0.5rem`).
- AI messages sit on luminous blush glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` with backdrop blur), accented with a left-edge gradient trace flowing from coral (`#FF6F61`) into warm amber (`#FBBF24`).
